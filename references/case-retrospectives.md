# Case retrospectives

This document records the 5 promo videos delivered with this tool. The finished videos and project files are not distributed with the repository (they are large and contain their own assets and keys), so only the parts useful for future projects are kept here: why each video chose its style, what feedback the user gave, and how it was ultimately fixed.

Read this to learn methods. **Do not copy any video's colors, layout, shots, or effects**; the next product needs its own answer.

---

## 1. Vibe Coding course promo video

- **Product**: a free beginner course on AI coding.
- **Finished video**: dark movie-trailer style, 78 seconds, 4K60, with a web playback version.
- **History**: 4 rounds of feedback. The first version had 0.45-second fast cuts and 3.6-second information scenes and was called "too fast, the audience can't keep up," which is where the "pacing floors" in AGENTS.md came from. The opening music cut in mid-phrase and was padded with a synthesized low-frequency drone and called "weird music," which is where the "enter on a bar line, no drone padding" rule came from. The user also pointed out that "nowhere in the whole film is the full URL shown," so the URL must appear at the highest climax.
- **What to learn**: rebuilding the real application interface as layered React components so it "grows" into place piece by piece is far more vivid than pasting in a static screenshot.

## 2. Codefather promo video

- **Product**: a programming-learning community with learning paths, project tutorials, interview questions, and a discussion area.
- **Finished video**: light isometric 3D navigation map style (an SVG city drawn in code), 60 seconds, 4K60 + 1080p60 + web version.
- **History**: the second version was rebuilt entirely in response to "slow pacing, missing real screenshots, music that doesn't fit." The rebuild opened on a pain point, entered the music drop at 3.9 seconds, captured 10 real 4K pages after logging in, and switched the picture every 2 beats in the project montage. The user also pointed out that the learning-path segment showed a single detail page and "should show the learning path list page," because the sheer quantity is itself a selling point. From the third version on, the music, sound effects, and narration were all generated by ElevenLabs.
- **What to learn**: after ElevenLabs composes from the storyboard, use the `music` stitch table to lengthen the track on bar lines; the narration used Haoran from the voice library (`pU9NaAwkoR3v0Mrg3uKz`, the "advertising" category, deep and resonant), and among three candidates only its opening line needed no speed-up. The URL transcribed correctly on its own but was heard as "点 CM" once mixed into the music; it only stabilized after deepening the duck on that line, softening the flash sound effect at the same moment, and writing the `say` as a capitalized "C N."

The real shape of the music and narration in `timing.json` (excerpt):

```json
{
  "beat": 0.5453, "bar": 2.1812, "drop": 4.3624, "musicOffset": 3.3696,
  "music": [
    {"at": 0, "from": 3.3696, "to": 55.7184},
    {"at": 52.3488, "from": 46.9936, "to": 51.356},
    {"at": 56.7112, "from": 55.7184, "to": 58.56}
  ],
  "voice": {
    "voiceId": "pU9NaAwkoR3v0Mrg3uKz", "model": "eleven_v4", "language": "zh",
    "settings": {"stability": 0.45, "similarity_boost": 0.75, "use_speaker_boost": true},
    "rmsDb": -13, "duckDb": -9, "gain": 1.0,
    "lines": [
      {"id": "hook", "at": 0.35, "end": 4.25, "text": "从哪开始学？项目没亮点？面试总卡壳？"},
      {"id": "brand", "at": 4.65, "end": 8.6, "text": "编程导航，学路线、做项目、拿 Offer！"}
    ]
  }
}
```

The `music` table means: starting at second `at` of the video, play the `from`–`to` span of the track. The second span repeats 2 bars from the middle so the track just fills 60 seconds, with every stitch point landing on a bar line.

## 3. Mianshiya promo video

- **Product**: a coding-interview practice platform.
- **Finished video**: black-and-white action manga style (a single duck-yellow accent color), version 2 is 53.7 seconds, 4K60 + 1080p60 + web version.
- **History**: version 2 was rebuilt in response to "slow pacing, missing real screenshots, unrealistic AI questions, music that doesn't fit." It opens by firing off the 4 real questions with the most views on the site; ElevenLabs composed from the storyboard (two drops + a breakdown in the middle, with the answer narration during the breakdown). The narration used the author's own cloned voice, which was trained only for `eleven_multilingual_v2`; at stability 0.5, "一万多道题" was heard as "一模多刀," and only at 0.85 did it stabilize.
- **What to learn**: AI-generated manga panels were 4× upscaled with Real-ESRGAN so the lines would not go soft at 4K; 283 real question-bank icons were scraped through an official public endpoint into an icon wall; screenshots were placed inside a manga ink-frame container and pushed and pulled, getting both style and real screenshots. There was also a lesson: "再精挑" was heard as "带金条" in the mix; at the time it was thought that the riser sound effect was covering the voice, so the sound effect was deleted, but a later comparison showed that what actually fixed it was regenerating a take with clearer articulation (see section 5 of `narration.md`).

## 4. Yupi AI Navigation promo video

- **Product**: a site that catalogs AI tools and resources.
- **Finished video**: bright underwater chibi style (a little fish mascot based on the logo + sticker outlines), 66.3 seconds, 4K60 + 1080p60 + music-only version + web version.
- **History**: this one went through 5 rounds and had the most problems, and it also left behind the most rules:
  - Slow opening: the pain-point section was cut from 9.3 seconds to 4 seconds.
  - Missing site screenshots: each section used a browser window scrolling a real page. The knowledge-base tutorials required a login to see the full text, so after the user logged in via the AI tool's built-in browser, a snapshot re-render produced the 4K image (`snapshot.js` was written at this point).
  - Music rejected twice when chosen by the AI: version 1 picked the childlike "Kids," version 2 switched to an upbeat pop track, and neither fit. The third time, the styles were widened to 6 candidates layered with narration, and the user settled on Mixkit's "Better Times are Coming" in one round. The lesson is that music follows the audience and the product, not the art style.
  - Screenshots squashed: Tailwind's `max-width: 100%` on `img` squeezed the enlarged screenshots horizontally; the template's `index.css` has been fixed.
  - Brand name mispronounced + echo + wrong timbre: `multilingual_v2` ran "鱼皮 AI" together into "鱼排," with a 90–360 ms reverb tail at the end of the sentence; switching to `eleven_v3` fixed the pronunciation, and converting back to the person's own timbre with speech-to-speech fixed the rest (`voice.sts` and `voice.keywords` were both added at this point).
- **Note**: this project was copied from an old template and its narration did not go through `vo`. Future projects should always use the template's `elevenlabs.py vo` and never write their own generation script.

## 5. Claude Fable 5.5 promo video

- **Product**: an AI model version not yet officially released, circulating only in a limited rollout among users.
- **Finished video**: leaked-dossier style (charcoal black + kraft paper + ochre orange), 60 seconds, 4K60 + 1080p60 + web version. This is an "early look": an "unofficially confirmed" label stays on screen throughout.
- **History**: 3 rounds of feedback. The author's own cloned voice was called "a lot of the tones are a bit off," and after switching to Haoran from the voice library, the mix transcript score rose from 0.968 to 1.000; the opening used a riddle-style hook like "it's labeled 5.1, but the answer isn't it?" which people without the background could not follow, so it was changed to tell the story in the order "what it is → how it was discovered → how it was verified → the work → officially unconfirmed"; the first 4 seconds packed 3 shots and pushed two focal points in 2 seconds and were called "too fast, no time to see the picture," so it was changed to 2 shots in the first 4 seconds with each focal point holding at least 1.4 seconds.
- **What to learn**: every case was traced back to the original X post, with `xgrab.py` downloading the original images and videos, and tweets could be captured without logging in; each case was labeled `@author · date`, and the ending listed all sources. The composition was planned with a quiet passage that the model did not deliver, so `audiolib.muffle` low-passed those bars to create the "officials haven't spoken yet" silence.

## Common patterns

Looking at the feedback across all 5 videos, the things users cared about most were always these:

1. **Open fast.** Almost every video's first round of feedback was "the opening is slow." The pain point or highlight must appear within 3 seconds.
2. **Show real things.** Redrawn cards and AI-generated images, however polished, cannot replace real screenshots and real work.
3. **The user must hear the music for themselves.** The AI's own music picks were rejected more than anything else; the earlier you offer a listen, the less trouble later.
4. **Clarity beats suspense.** An opening an outsider cannot follow is useless no matter how clever insiders find it.
5. **A cloned voice is not necessarily the best choice.** The user's own cloned voice often has odd tones and unsteady articulation; put it side by side with voice-library voices and let the user decide.
